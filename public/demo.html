Under the hood

Three steps. About thirty seconds.

visibility

Step 1

Vision model reads the x-ray

A multimodal model identifies missing teeth, caries, restorations, bone-density signals, and any structural concerns visible in the panoramic image.

description

Step 2

Treatment plan generated

The findings get mapped to standard procedure codes (implant, crown, root canal, etc.) and ordered by clinical priority and dependency.

compare_arrows

Step 3

Cost comparison rendered

US averages (CareCredit/ADA fee surveys) vs. our partner-clinic rate cards in Tijuana, Algodones, Cancún, Vallarta. Always shown as a range.

info Every AI plan is reviewed by a partner dentist before it reaches a patient. AI drafts; humans clinically validate.
