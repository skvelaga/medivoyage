What the AI does — and what the dentist does

The AI is a pre-screener, not a diagnostician. It reads your case, drafts a provisional briefing, and flags clinical considerations. Dr. Rivas at PV Smile then reviews the briefing, confirms or edits the plan, and locks pricing. v1 scope: single missing tooth (implant + crown). Other cases are flagged for direct dentist review.
