FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why is dental work so much cheaper in Mexico? add
The cost difference is primarily driven by lower lab costs, lower administrative overhead, and the absence of high-interest student loans for medical professionals. Quality is not the variable — our partner clinics use the same FDA-approved implant systems, the same imaging equipment, and the same lab materials as US clinics.
Are the materials and lab work the same as in the US? add
Yes. We exclusively use world-leading brands like Straumann, Nobel Biocare, and Dentsply Sirona. Every partner receives a certification of authenticity for the implants used and the materials in any restoration.
How do you vet the partner clinics? add
Every clinic in our concierge network undergoes a 100-point verification process, including credential review (ADA, AACD, ABOI, ICOI memberships), board certifications, equipment standards (Planmeca, Sirona), sterilization protocol audits, and case-volume thresholds. Only partners with the top 5% of Mexico's dentistry get onboarded.
What happens if there's a complication after I return home? add
We do voice and text aftercare check-ins on day 3, 7, and 14. If anything looks off in your follow-up photos, we coordinate with both your partner clinic and a local US dentist for emergency care if needed. Implant warranties are passed through from the manufacturer (typically 10+ years) and your partner clinic backs their workmanship for the same period.
How long do I need to be in Mexico? add
For a single implant, plan on 3–5 days in Mexico for placement, then return 3–6 months later for 2–3 days for the final crown. Single-implant cases at our Tijuana partner clinics can sometimes be completed same-day-return via the San Ysidro crossing if no bone grafting is required.
