Why Mexico

Same crowns. Same implants.
Different price tag.

The Mexican Health Ministry estimates 1.2 million Americans cross the border every year for dental care. Medivoyage is the AI agent that orchestrates that journey end-to-end.

The cost gap is real

A single implant + crown that runs $5,000+ in the US averages $1,500–$2,000 at our partner clinics — using the same Straumann and Nobel Biocare systems your US dentist quotes by name.

How it works

Five steps, no surprises.

01

Tell us your case

Share an x-ray and a few photos. Our AI flags the procedure type and complexity.

02

Get a treatment plan

A first-draft plan with US vs. Mexico cost comparison, reviewed by a partner dentist within 24 hours.

03

We pick the clinic

Matched by specialty and case fit — not bid auctions. One clinic, one quote, locked.

04

We book the trip

Flights, hotel, ground transport, translation if needed. You bring a passport and show up.

05

Aftercare in English

Voice and text check-ins post-op so you're never on hold with an international clinic.
